---
title: Legendary Pokémon
description: Finding legendary Pokémon on the Kanto scanner.
---

Kanto identifies Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Mewtwo and Mew as legendary Pokémon.
In a Pokémon filter, choose **Any**, **Legendary Pokémon**, or **Non-legendary
Pokémon**. This combines with species, shiny and other existing filter settings.

Legendary markers have a gold halo and ring. Their gold countdown is always
visible while the marker is displayed and uses hours, minutes and seconds from
the server's expiry time. Other Pokémon keep their existing optional timers.
Expired Pokémon disappear through the normal map lifecycle.

The Kanto API supplies a `legendary` flag. The five Gen 1 species are also
recognised when connected to an older Kanto server that omits that flag.
